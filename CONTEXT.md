# ROOM audio playground

The playground lets listeners perform changes to recorded ROOM mixes and hand a musical idea to someone else.

## Language

**Passage** — The exact range of a shareable ROOM mix selected for a performance.

**Gesture** — A timed action within one experiment that a maker can perform, add, move or remove.

**Take** — One experiment performed over one passage, with the gestures and sound needed to hear and edit the performance. A shared take keeps its own version.

**Variation** — A take made by editing a copy of an existing take.

**Fragment** — A piece of captured source audio that a Memory gesture plays. Moving the gesture keeps the captured material.
